How to play

The idea: an outbreak hits your real town at 7:00 AM. You have only what's in your pockets. Survive until the evacuation helicopters arrive on Day 3, 7 to 11 AM.

1. Set up

  • Type a zip code or town, or tap 📍 Use my current location, and pick an area size. With your location, your start point is set to where you really are, and you can tap the map to move it.
  • Choose what you're surviving: Zombies (bites infect you), Toxic Gas (the cloud hurts you every hour you're in it, and masks help), or Something Else (hiding works better than fighting).
  • Pick up to 6 pocket items, or type your own. Typed items get a role from their name: "pepper spray" becomes a weapon and distraction, "headlamp" becomes a light, "jerky" becomes food.

2. Place the scene

  • Tap the map where you are when it starts, then Confirm.
  • Tap where the outbreak begins, then Start. It has to be a few blocks away from you.

3. Survive

  • Tap a place marker to see its danger and walking time, then hit Go here. Walking takes time and energy.
  • The colored circle is the danger zone. It grows every hour. Without a phone or radio, your view of it only updates every 6 hours.
  • Scavenge (1 hour): each kind of place has its own loot. Hardware stores have weapons and tools, pharmacies have meds, police stations have a gun and ammo, gas stations have flares and power banks. Places run dry after a few searches, and searching at night without a light finds less.
  • Rest or Sleep to get energy back. Sleeping in a dangerous spot can get you woken up by something.
  • Use food and water from your inventory. When food or water hits 0, you start losing health.
  • You can carry 8 things. A backpack adds 8 more slots.

Type what you do

  • At any location, type an action in What do you do here?, like "block the doors with the shelves," "climb on the roof and look around," "search the back for medicine," or "tape my knife to a broom."
  • The AI game master decides what happens from where you are, what you're carrying and how dangerous it is. If the AI is off or busy, the game handles common actions itself.
  • Barricades (up to 3) make it less likely things find you there, and give you an edge if they do. They weaken over time.
  • Scouting makes you alert, which lowers your encounter chance for 3 hours.
  • Noisy actions, like a fire, attract trouble.

On the road

  • Every trip tells you what happened on the way: wrecks, abandoned cars, survivors, strange noises, barking dogs. Some give you a choice.
  • Feed a stray dog and it becomes your companion 🐕. It warns you of danger, which lowers your encounter chance.

4. Encounters

  • Choose Fight, Run, Distract or Hide. Your odds are shown on each button. While traveling, you can also find another way around, which costs time. If they spot you, you'll have to deal with them.
  • Or type your own move, like "throw my keys and climb onto the car." Mentioning an item you're carrying improves your odds.
  • Your best weapon is used automatically. A gun is strongest but uses ammo.
  • Running costs energy. Hiding works better at night. Distracting uses up firecrackers and flares.

5. Evacuation

  • With a phone or radio, you'll hear the evac location on Day 1 night. Without one, you won't find out until Day 2 afternoon.
  • Reach the 🚁 marker and press Wait for evac. Miss the window and you're left behind.

Danger levels: low moderate high overrun safe

This is a test build. Real places come from OpenStreetMap, so small towns may have fewer of them. If loading fails, wait a minute and try again.
